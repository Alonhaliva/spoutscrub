How it works

Three moves. Zero toothbrushes.

01 · PULL

Pull the gasket tab

The pick lifts the silicone ring in one motion, without tearing it. Works on FreeSip, FlowState and Flex Straw gaskets.

02 · SLIDE

Slide the sleeve over the spout

Internal ribs grip the mouthpiece and scrub every side at once. Twice down, twice up. The gasket goes in the sleeve's cup for the same treatment.

03 · RINSE

Rinse, stand it up, walk away

Open bottom drains and dries. Top-rack dishwasher safe. Snap the gasket back, done before your coffee is.

Questions

Fair questions.

Does it actually fit my Owala?

The sleeve's inner ribs are tuned to the FreeSip's dual spout and stretch to any round or oval mouthpiece up to 32 mm across. Tell us your bottle in the form and we'll confirm fit before you're ever charged.

Is this just a brush?

No. A brush cleans one line at a time and needs the lid torn down. The sleeve scrubs the whole spout at once, the gasket cup does the seal, and the channel brush is only for the sip channel. Total time is about 20 seconds.

Does it kill mold?

It removes the buildup you can see and the biofilm that causes the smell. We don't make disinfectant claims. Pair it with hot water, or a cleaning tablet if you like, and clean weekly.
