The problem

The lid is the hard part.

The inside of a bottle is easy: a brush and some soap. The lid is different. The spout, the seal and the small gaps around them collect buildup, and reaching it usually means taking the lid apart.

In a small survey we ran with bottle owners in early 2026, more than half named part of the lid (the seal, the crevices or the spout) as the hardest thing to clean.Our own survey, January–February 2026.
How it works

Three steps, no tools.

STEP 1

Slide it on

Put the sleeve over the spout. It's soft silicone, designed to stretch over spouts up to 32 mm wide.

STEP 2

Scrub

Move it up and down a few times. The ribs inside rub the outside of the spout clean.

STEP 3

Rinse

Rinse the spout and the sleeve. Use the small pick for the seal groove.

INSIDE THE SLEEVE

Ribs do the scrubbing.

This cutaway removes the front of the sleeve. The thin ribs on the inside wall touch the spout all the way around, so one slide cleans the whole surface at once.
